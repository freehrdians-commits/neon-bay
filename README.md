Neon Bay is an open-world crime game that runs in a desktop browser from static files. Rook is fresh out of the east-side lockup, and Delia Vane — who runs a garage on the waterfront — has work for him. Explore a sun-soaked 5×5-block city on foot and by car, steal cars off the street, outrun the police as your wanted level climbs, and run four story jobs: pick up a package at the docks, steal a gold Comet GT from the Vipers, take back Crescent Plaza in a turf war, and lift Marlow's ledger off his courier — while the police cut you off with intercept driving and roadblocks. Between jobs, hunt the eight hidden neon tags, use nitro (Shift) to outrun a chase, and watch the city change from day to dusk to neon night. The city has traffic that keeps its lane, pedestrians on the sidewalks, a day/night cycle with street lamps and headlights, synthesized sound, a HUD with a minimap, and a save system. Every character, place, vehicle and story beat is original.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser. No build step, no network requests.

Controls: WASD or arrows to move and drive · Shift for nitro while driving · E to enter a car or interact · F to punch · Click to shoot · Esc to pause · S to save while paused.
